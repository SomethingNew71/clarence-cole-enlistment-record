The Timeline

Battalion events in ink, the wider war in grey. Each entry names the microfilm frame it was read from. The routine days between — in position firing and nothing more — are in the Archive.

Lines marked modelled are the weather, and are the one thing here not read off the film. The cards never mention it. Those figures come from ERA5, a modern reconstruction of past weather, for the day and the region — the method is set out in the Archive.
