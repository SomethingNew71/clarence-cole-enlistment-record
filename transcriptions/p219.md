The film photographed this page twice. Frame 219 carries the same two cards as
frame 218 — 11 July and 12 July 1945, Btry C at Herzhausen G8188 — and is the
cleaner of the two exposures. Rows are not repeated here; they live on p218.

Frame 218 is faint enough that two fields were misread from it on the first pass
and corrected against this exposure: the officer joining on 11 July is
**Mulcahy**, not Malcolm, and his MOS column reads **1183**, distinct from the
`Duty 1193` written in the body of the same entry.
